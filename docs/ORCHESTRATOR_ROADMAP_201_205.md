# Orchestrator Roadmap 201-205

## Tranche goal

Convert the admitted three-step canary surface into real supervised execution proof.

This tranche is not about broadening claims quickly. It is about proving one more narrow widening step honestly, with durable artifacts and explicit controller / supervision truth.

## Task list

- 201 — supervised three-step canary runner and durable chain ledger
- 202 — curated three-step canary corpus and manifest truth
- 203 — three-step canary benchmark and supervision-aware scorecard
- 204 — controller route trace and resume reconstruction for chained canary runs
- 205 — supervised multi-task canary checkpoint and adjacent-manifest gate

## Exit criteria

The tranche is successful only if it produces:

- a real exactly-three-task canary runner,
- a curated and durable canary corpus,
- a benchmark path that measures direct vs supervised chain progress explicitly,
- durable controller-route and resume truth across chained runs,
- and a checkpoint that states whether a tiny adjacent-manifest gate is justified under supervision.

## Non-goals

- Do not claim unattended multi-task autonomy.
- Do not jump from the bounded two-task pilot to arbitrary task lists.
- Do not widen beyond an explicitly supervised adjacent canary shape.
- Do not reopen standalone productization work.
